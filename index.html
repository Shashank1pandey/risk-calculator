Lot size is always rounded DOWN to the lot step. Pip = 0.0001 (0.01 for JPY pairs). Gold: 1 pip = 0.10 by default ($10/pip/lot); some traders count 0.01 — change it in Settings. Indices & ETH: 1 pip = 1.0 price point; BTC: 1 pip = 10.0. Entering entry+SL prices gives the right $ risk regardless of pip convention.
